ADULLAM INTERNATIONAL MINISTRIES

the real victories are changed lives

CHANGE A LIFE

MORE THAN JUST A RESCUE PLAN

We are a non-profit organization based in Wetumpka, Alabama and our primary mission is to further the Kingdom of God by providing a safe haven for at-risk children and their families. We started out with one small but passionate dream; to rescue babies born in prison to incarcerated mothers.

Today our vision is so much more than just a rescue plan!

Learn more

TODAY’S GLOBAL MISSION

The global mission of Adullam International Ministries (AIM) is to not only provide a safe-haven for vulnerable children - from new-born-babies, through to teens and young adults – but to also help establish, equip, educate and empower these precious souls, enabling them to embrace their own unique God-given mission.

Not only do we get to step right into the middle of their broken stories and introduce them to Jesus, we get to watch as He rewrites the endings too!

We currently operate non-profits in the US, Moldova, and Kenya, with a further key partnership in Canada.

OUR MISSION AND VALUES

Our Reach:

  • Adullam House

    Adullam House provides a safe haven for children of incarcerated parents.

    Learn More

  • AIM Academy

    AIM Academy provides high quality, Christian education for the resident children of Adullam House and the surrounding communities.

    Learn More

  • AIM Moldova

    AIM Moldova provides care, education, and life skills to protect vulnerable kids from human traffickers.

    Learn More

  • AIM Church

    AIM Church is a diverse place of worship and fellowship for the Adullam House children and Wetumpka community.

    Learn More

  • AIM Thrift

    The AIM Thrift Store is a place for the local community to donate, shop, and volunteer. 100% of the profits support AIM!

    Learn More

  • AIM Kenya

    In a short time, AIM has already provided a school, access to clean water, medical clinics and evangelism to communities across 60 remote villages.

Our AIM Heroes

The dream team

The ministry of AIM depends upon our team of dedicated, caring, and compassionate people. Pete and Angie Spackman, along with the AIM Staff and volunteers, provide an environment of hope and purpose and direction in which children can flourish and grow. 

From the beginning, each volunteer from the workers, builders, and caregivers have rallied behind the mission of AIM to make each dream a reality.
